Backup

Export downloads a full backup of entries, budgets, categories and settings. Import merges a backup into this device (newest edit wins). The exported file is plain readable JSON, not encrypted — keep it somewhere private, and treat it as your safety net if you ever lose both the passcode and the recovery key.

Passcode & encryption

Your ledger is encrypted in this browser before it is saved or synced, using a key that only your passcode (or your recovery key) can unwrap. Nobody who reads this page's source — and nobody with access to the database — can read your entries without it. Changing the passcode re-wraps that key and leaves your data untouched.

A 6-digit code is convenient but low-entropy. Someone who obtains the encrypted copy could grind through all million combinations offline; the encryption is deliberately slow to make that expensive, not impossible. Keep the recovery key private, and export a backup now and then.

Danger zone
